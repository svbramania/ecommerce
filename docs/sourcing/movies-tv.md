## Sourcing model

This store has no distribution deal with any studio, so it cannot legitimately
sell copies of current, licensed studio films or TV shows. All 12 products are
real, currently-catalogued DVD releases of films that have lapsed into the
U.S. public domain, put out by **Alpha Video** (Alpha Home Entertainment), a
real, long-running label (5,100+ titles released since 2001) that specializes
exclusively in manufacturing and marketing public-domain films and TV on DVD.
Each Alpha Video catalog number below was confirmed via real third-party
retail listings (oldies.com), which mirror Alpha Video's own catalog numbers
and UPCs; oldies.com's product pages themselves returned HTTP 403 to
automated fetches (consistent with the bot-protection issue flagged by the
earlier attempt), so no retailer page was scraped — only public search-result
metadata was used for citation, and cover art was sourced independently from
Wikimedia Commons.

Cover art for every product is the film's **original theatrical release
poster**, sourced from Wikimedia Commons, where each file is tagged with a
public-domain license (`PD-US-no-notice`, `PD-US-not-renewed`, or equivalent —
confirmed individually per file via the Commons API before download, not
assumed). No competitor product photography and no Alpha Video packaging art
was used.

No fake movie titles were invented, no current studio film's title or cover
art was used, and no accessory products were needed since 12 legitimate
public-domain-film DVDs were found.
