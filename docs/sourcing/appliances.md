## Notes on sourcing method

- Alibaba.com's search/listing page (`https://www.alibaba.com/trade/search?SearchText=...`) returned an immediate slider CAPTCHA on first navigation, so per the standing constraint (never solve a CAPTCHA anywhere) Alibaba was abandoned entirely for this batch and **Made-in-China.com** was used instead — the same legitimate public B2B marketplace used for the prior Pet Supplies batch, whose category listing pages show thumbnail images, titles, and MOQ price ranges with no bot-check.
- Only listing/search-results pages were used; no product-detail page was opened on any site, and no CAPTCHA was solved anywhere.
- All 12 thumbnail images were downloaded from `image.made-in-china.com` listing thumbnails, converted from WebP to JPEG, and saved to `/tmp/product-images/<slug>.jpg` before upload to Saleor via `productMediaCreate`.
- Two categories (items 6/11) required a follow-up, more specific search query after the first query's listing page returned unrelated results (a client-side "recommended products" carousel occasionally replaced the visible grid a few seconds after page load); re-navigating to the same or a refined URL and re-reading the DOM immediately resolved this each time.
- All descriptions above (in the Saleor product records) were written from scratch based on general knowledge of each product category and the cited trend research — none were copied from any supplier or competitor listing.
- Products deliberately use generic/descriptive names (e.g. "Digital Air Fryer, 6.3L") rather than the specific branded models named in research articles (e.g. Cosori, Breville, Instant Pot), since sourcing is from generic/OEM B2B suppliers rather than those brands directly.
