## Notes on sourcing method

- Alibaba.com's search/listing page itself returned a slider CAPTCHA on first navigation (`https://www.alibaba.com/trade/search?SearchText=...`), so per the standing constraint (never solve a CAPTCHA anywhere) Alibaba was abandoned entirely for this batch and **Made-in-China.com** was used instead — a legitimate public B2B supplier marketplace whose category listing pages show thumbnail images, titles, and MOQ price ranges with no bot-check.
- Only listing/search-results pages were used; no product-detail page was opened on any site.
- All 10 thumbnail images were downloaded from `image.made-in-china.com` listing thumbnails, converted from WebP to JPEG, and saved to `/tmp/product-images/<slug>.jpg` before upload to Saleor via `productMediaCreate`.
- All descriptions above (in the Saleor product records) were written from scratch based on general knowledge of each product category and the cited trend research — none were copied from any supplier or competitor listing.
