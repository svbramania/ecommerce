# Amazon Devices & Accessories category — sourcing notes (2026-09-14)

Documents how the 10 products added to the "Amazon Devices & Accessories"
category (Saleor category ID `Q2F0ZWdvcnk6MzI=`) were picked and sourced, so
the "real, currently-trending, non-infringing" claims are traceable.

**Category-count note:** the target was 12 products; only **10** legitimate,
clearly generic, non-infringing products could be sourced with a real
reference image and a real market price. Searches for several additional
sub-categories came back empty or irrelevant on the available supplier
sites — replacement micro-USB/USB-C charging cables for streaming sticks (no
results distinct enough from generic phone cables), universal e-reader
stylus pens (only Apple Pencil–branded or generic tablet-case results),
under-cabinet smart-speaker mounts (results were all pro-audio/AV
equipment), clip-on e-reader reading lights (results were all sunglasses,
an unrelated keyword collision), and a second adjustable floor stand for
smart speakers (only 163 results, none genuinely on-topic). Per the
assignment's explicit instruction, the category was stopped at 10 rather
than padded with tenuous or fabricated items.

**Naming/branding note:** this category is named after the real Amazon.com
department for taxonomy-matching purposes only. This store does not sell
Amazon-brand hardware (Echo, Kindle, Fire TV, Alexa). Every product below is
a generic, unbranded THIRD-PARTY accessory designed to work with
smart-speaker, e-reader, or streaming-device ecosystems in general. No
product name, description, or listing anywhere below uses the words
"Amazon," "Echo," "Kindle," "Alexa," or "Fire TV," and none is a copy or
counterfeit of Amazon's own hardware.

**Images:** Alibaba's search-results pages hit an immediate slider CAPTCHA
in this environment (confirmed on the very first search attempt) — per the
non-negotiable rules, this was not bypassed. Sourcing moved to
**DHgate.com**, whose search/listing pages were reachable without any bot
check. All images below are listing-page thumbnails pulled directly from
DHgate search results; no product-detail page was ever opened. Several
reference photos incidentally show a round/dome-shaped smart speaker (to
demonstrate fit) or a third-party e-reader brand name like "PocketBook" —
these are not Amazon products, and the product actually offered in every
case is the generic mount/case/cable/cover itself, not the device shown for
scale. Where a listing's thumbnail showed a specific branded device
prominently and a cleaner generic alternative existed in the same result
set, the generic one was chosen (e.g. Product 1 and Product 3 below).
