# CDs & Vinyl — Sourcing Documentation

Category ID: `Q2F0ZWdvcnk6MTk=` ("CDs & Vinyl"). 12 of 12 target products created.

## Sourcing model

This category cannot legitimately sell copies of currently-charting, major-label
albums — the store has no distribution deal with any label. Instead, the 12
products split into two legitimate sourcing lanes:

1. **Public-domain classical/early-recording reissues** (6 products) — real,
   currently catalogued CD reissues from Naxos Historical, a real label that
   specializes in restoring recordings whose underlying performances are in
   the public domain (acoustic-era recordings from 1902-1936). Cover art is
   taken directly from Naxos's own public catalogue pages (cdn.naxos.com),
   cited per item below.
2. **Generic vinyl-care/storage accessories** (6 products) — physical
   accessories with no copyrighted musical content, sourced the same way any
   other generic-supplier accessory in this store is sourced: via
   Made-in-China.com search-listing pages (never a product detail page,
   never behind a CAPTCHA). Images are the listing-page thumbnails for the
   cited listing.

No fake "generic" album titles were invented, and no real current artist's
name, likeness, or cover art was used without a distribution right.
