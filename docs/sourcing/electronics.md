# Electronics category — sourcing notes (2026-09-13)

Documents how the 11 new products added to the Electronics category
(Saleor category ID `Q2F0ZWdvcnk6MTM=`) were picked and sourced, so the
"real, currently-trending" claim is traceable.

**Category-count note:** the assignment brief said Electronics already had 2
products (a stylus pen and a Wi-Fi smart plug) and asked for 10 more to reach
12. A query at the start of this pass (`category(id: "Q2F0ZWdvcnk6MTM=")`)
showed only 1 product actually in Electronics — the Fine-Tip Stylus Pen. The
"Smart Plug (Wi-Fi, No Hub Required)" (`SMART-PLUG-01`) is filed under the
**Home & Kitchen** category, not Electronics. Per the "don't touch other
categories" rule, it was left where it is rather than moved. To reach the
stated goal of 12 total products in Electronics, **11** new products were
added instead of 10.

**Images:** Alibaba's search/listing pages hit a slider CAPTCHA immediately
in this environment (not just the product-detail pages), so sourcing moved
to **Made-in-China.com**, whose category listing pages are reachable without
any bot check. All images below are listing-page thumbnails from that site;
no product-detail page was opened and no CAPTCHA was solved anywhere. Where
a listing page showed both generic, unbranded items and items imitating a
specific brand (e.g. "AirPods Pro copy" earbuds, "TP-Link Tapo" camera
clones), the generic/unbranded listing was used instead.
