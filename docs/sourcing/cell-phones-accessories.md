## Sourcing notes
- Alibaba.com was attempted first per the category instructions but returned a slider CAPTCHA ("Please drag the slider to verify") on the trade-search page; per the non-negotiable rules this was not solved, and sourcing fell back to Made-in-China.com search-results pages for all 12 products.
- Images were pulled directly from the thumbnail images shown on each search-results page (not a detail/listing page), so resolution is limited to the site's listing-grid thumbnail size (~160–300px). This is a direct consequence of only sourcing from search-result pages as instructed.
- Two initial thumbnail picks were rejected after visual review: one mismatched CDN thumbnail unrelated to the product, and one showing a supplier's own logo/brand watermark; both were replaced with clean, generic alternates from a re-run search before use.
- No Apple, Samsung, or other device-brand logos or trade dress appear in any of the 12 final images; all copy was written from scratch describing generic/universal compatibility ("fits most smartphones," "USB-C," "Qi-enabled") rather than any specific branded phone model.
